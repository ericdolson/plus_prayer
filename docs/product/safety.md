# Safety — self-harm, threats, child safety

_Last updated: 2026-10-18. **KNOWN GAP** — not built for the private beta beyond
the items marked "beta". Close before public launch (roadmap Stage 2). Not legal
advice; final policy and terms need lawyer review._

## Why this matters
Intentions are free text in the user's own words. Some will express self-harm
("Strength to not hurt myself") or, rarely, harm toward others. Eric reads the
new/changed diff each morning (with an AI first pass), so +Prayer will sometimes
have actual knowledge of this content.

## Legal context to confirm with counsel (as understood 2026-10-18)
- US: no general duty for an app to monitor private content; Section 230 broadly
  shields platforms from liability for user content; no general "duty to warn"
  (that applies to therapists). Federal law **permits** emergency disclosure to
  law enforcement when there's danger of death or serious injury.
- **Child sexual abuse material: providers with knowledge must report to NCMEC.**
- EU (DSA): a hosting service aware of information suggesting a criminal offence
  threatening life/safety must notify authorities — relevant once EU users exist.
- App Store / Google Play: user-generated-content apps are expected to filter
  objectionable content and offer reporting/blocking — mostly relevant once
  groups or the public feed exist, but expect review questions.

## DECIDED approach
- **Self-harm language: never block, reject, or shame.** Praying is a healthy
  response. The intention saves and can print. A quiet, dismissable resource card
  appears: "If you're struggling, you don't have to carry this alone." with the
  988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US) and an international helpline directory.
  Detection runs **on device** (no extra data sent anywhere). Card wording needs
  Eric's approval. — Stage 2
- **Harm toward others** (threats against a named person, wishing harm): not
  printed (excluded, "Didn't meet list guidelines"); prohibited by the terms;
  credible, imminent threats (specific person + plan) → contact local law
  enforcement per the internal policy. — beta (manual via Eric's review)
- **Child-safety content:** report to NCMEC per the internal policy. — beta
  (manual)
